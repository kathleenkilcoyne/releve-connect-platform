## The core distinction (this solves a lot)

**Every vetted Relevé professional CAN license work. Not every work becomes a
Relevé-*curated* work.** Relevé doesn't decide whether an artist *deserves* to
sell — it gives vetted artists the infrastructure to put their work into commerce.
**The marketplace decides what sells.**

## Two gates — not a heavy curation wall

Licensing is open to vetted professionals, protected by two light gates:

1. **The person is vetted** — Roster approval (already exists).
2. **Each work passes a *lightweight* licensing review** before it appears
   publicly — rights attestation, a basic quality floor, honest tagging,
   age-appropriateness. This keeps Relevé from becoming "Etsy for random
   choreography" *without* pretending to hand-curate every piece.

Then a subset earns the **✦ Relevé Signature Selection** badge — the difference
between *being allowed on the shelf* and *being placed in the front window*.
**Senior Spotlight is one of those front windows** — a highly curated collection
drawn from the larger ecosystem, not the entire licensing system.

## Splits reflect WHAT RELEVÉ DOES for the work — not the artist's fame

This replaces the Emerging/Established/Signature *fame* ladder, which created the
"why does hers earn 80% and mine 60% when we sit side by side on the Roster?"
problem. Tie the percentage to Relevé's role, which is explainable and fair.

| Tier | Artist / Relevé | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| **Roster Licensing** | **70 / 30** | Any vetted pro submits finished work; Relevé is the marketplace rail. No listing or sales guarantee, no upfront production obligation. The scalable marketplace. |
| **Signature / Curated** | **80 / 20** | Invitation-only. Relevé actively selects, presents, features, and puts its name behind work the artist delivers finished. |
| **Relevé Produced** | **60 / 40** | Only when Relevé does substantially more — filming, editing, packaging, music review, marketing assets, production. The larger Relevé share pays for the investment. NOT an "emerging artist" rate. |

### The one open number (Kathleen's call)
**Where does Senior Spotlight sit?** If Relevé **produces** it (films/edits/packages),
it's the **60/40 Produced** tier — which matches your instinct that 80/20 was too
generous for work *you* produce. If Relevé only **curates** finished work artists
deliver, it's **80/20**. You've said you'll be producing it → most likely **60/40**.
Decide and we lock it.

## Profile licensing section — the next build (before any checkout)

Put **licensing prominent near the TOP of the profile** (not buried under "More"),
beside teaching/coaching availability. For veteran artists it may be the single
most financially meaningful part of their Relevé presence.

- **Available for Licensing · On / Off** — a toggle (same pattern as Swing).
- When **ON** → a **"Works available to license"** section appears.
- **+ Add a Work** captures: title · work type · style · cast size · approx.
  duration · level / audience · year created · short description · preview video ·
  existing repertory vs newly created · type of licensing willing to offer.
- **Add does NOT publish.** It sets status = **Submitted for Relevé Review** →
  lightweight review → then public.
- A work may carry **✦ Relevé Signature Selection**.

**Three views to define (this IS the build):**
- **Artist sees:** their works + status (draft · submitted · in review · approved ·
  declined) + any badge.
- **Public sees:** only approved works — preview video + license terms; ✦ Signature
  Selection highlighted.
- **Relevé / admin sees:** the review queue — approve / return / decline, and elevate
  a work to Signature Selection / Senior Spotlight.

Build the section **visually and field-by-field first; checkout mechanics come
later.** Licensing stays "Coming" for actual transactions.

## What stays true (guardrails that don't move)

- **No-tax-on-labor** — revenue is the marketplace fee on a license, never a cut of
  the teaching or setting that happens after the sale.
- **Rights, not videos** — Relevé licenses the *choreography* as IP; music is the
  licensee's responsibility (AI / royalty-free guidance).
- **Original & owned** — the submitter warrants sole authorship and ownership.
- **Child-safety** — age-appropriate; extra care on anything involving minors.
- **Clean email discipline** — one confirmation on submit, one internal alert,
  approval emails only on explicit admin action.
