## The wedge — competition & college teams, NOT the whole studio
Relevé does **not** try to hold a studio's entire fall/spring rec calendar. That's a losing fight against the tools studios already pay for (Jackrabbit, Dance Studio Pro), it demands huge duplicate data entry, and it buries Relevé's value under rec-class noise nobody's anxious about.

Instead, Relevé owns the one slice those tools handle **worst** and where the pain is **sharpest**: the **competition team and college team** layer — shifting rehearsals, competition weekends, travel, auditions, deadlines, and the most engaged, most anxious families in the building. Narrow, specific, defensible. It keeps the build small AND the value dense: "This Week" for a comp family is *only* what matters this week, with no rec clutter diluting it.

## What Relevé deliberately does NOT do
- **No rec-calendar import.** Avoids the losing fight and the noise.
- **No studio advertising.** Holds the brand line — the demand side pays; studios and talent are never sold. Advertising would muddy the one thing that makes Relevé trustworthy.

## Scheduling scope — comp/college "kinds" only
`rehearsal · competition · audition · workshop/masterclass · performance · deadline/reminder`. **Not** weekly rec classes.

## The expansion, in earned order (each phase gated by faithful use of the last)
1. **The studio wedge (now):** comp/college calendar + families/dancers living in This Week.
2. **Advisory (later, earned):** after a studio uses Relevé faithfully, Relevé evaluates their **"white space."**
   ⚠️ **White-space caveat — write this down:** because Relevé deliberately does *not* hold the rec calendar, Advisory **cannot infer white space from the calendar.** When Advisory arrives it needs its **own light capacity snapshot** — rooms + open hours — provided by the studio *at the Advisory stage.* Never assume the calendar reveals white space; by design it doesn't.
3. **The Flex Series (after Advisory):** fill that white space with programming.

## Flex ≠ Swing (keep them architecturally separate)
- **Swing** fills a **teaching gap** — a sub is needed *now*.
- **Flex** fills a studio's **white space** — open capacity, filled with programming.
Same instinct ("fill the empty"), different trigger, different data, different phase. Do not weld them together.
