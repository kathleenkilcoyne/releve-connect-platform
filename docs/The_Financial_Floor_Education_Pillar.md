# The Financial Floor — Relevé Connect's Education Pillar
### Financial literacy for dance professionals, present and future.
*Captured 2026-07-26, from Kathleen — this is core to what Relevé Connect IS on the education side. It is not optional, and it is not a later "feature." It is a founding pillar. Do not leave it out of the vision.*

---

## Why this exists (the truth we start from)

Dancers stay poor. Not for lack of talent, not for lack of work ethic — the discipline
in this field is ferocious. They stay poor because **no one teaches them the money**, and
because the *shape* of a dancer's year is built to keep them broke if they don't understand it.

The structural reality, named plainly:

- **Dance income is seasonal.** Studios run roughly **September to June.** The calendar
  itself has holes in it.
- **The gaps are unpaid.** Vacations and holidays fall all through the year, and the work
  stops without the bills stopping.
- **It is inconsistent and rarely steady.** Year-round employment in one place is hard to
  find, so income arrives in bursts and then thins out — and nobody warned them it would.

A dancer is handed a career whose income doesn't look like a normal paycheck, and is never
taught to plan for it. That is the gap. **No tool has ever been built for this reality from
inside the dance world.** Relevé is positioned to be the first, because Relevé already lives
inside that world.

## What it is (the vision)

**Every Relevé customer gets access to financial literacy** — built for the actual economics
of a performing life. The goal is simple to say and rare to deliver:

> **Teach a dancer to see their own year — and then to plan it.**

To see the season and the gaps. To know that June's income has to carry July. To understand
saving, taxes on 1099 and mixed income, budgeting across an uneven year, and building toward
stability instead of surviving from burst to burst. This is the *floor* every performing
career should stand on — and almost none of them are given one.

It serves **the present professional and the future one.** The working teacher, choreographer,
and performer today — and the child, through their guardian's profile, learning
age-appropriately long before they earn a dollar.

## The lifecycle — it grows up with the dancer

This is where Relevé's architecture makes the pillar uniquely possible. A guardian manages a
young dancer's profile today; at 18 that same person converts to a professional account. So
financial literacy is **layered by age and delivered by the same platform across a lifetime:**

- **Child (guardian-managed):** age-appropriate money basics — value, saving, patience —
  surfaced to the guardian, never advice, always education.
- **Teen approaching 18:** the reality of a performing income before they're in it — the
  season, the gaps, the first taxes.
- **Working professional:** planning the uneven year, saving across the season, building
  toward steady ground.

The platform literally grows up alongside the dancer. That continuity is the moat — nobody
else is holding a hand from age ten to a working career.

## How it's built (rides on foundations already in place)

Financial literacy is **not a bolt-on.** It is the natural reading of information Relevé is
already collecting. The existing bones carry it with no rebuild:

- **The calendar spine already knows the seasonal rhythm** — the September-to-June shape and
  its gaps are *in the data.* That is the raw material of income planning.
- **The roster already knows the professional identity.**
- **Swing / Flex already know where the work is** — the income signal.
- **Guardian-managed minor profiles + the age-18 conversion** are the delivery vehicle for
  the age-layered curriculum.
- **Dashboard-by-role routing** means financial literacy becomes another surface, shaped for
  who signs in — light for a guardian, full for a professional.
- **Supabase (Postgres)** carries earnings tracking, seasonal income modeling, and a
  curriculum without outgrowing it.

**Build discipline:** lay it as an extension of the existing role + calendar + profile model,
the same lean-and-extensible way everything else was built. Foundations now; the full module
in its own time. Documented, brick by brick, with Claude Code.

## The programming — seed it from day one (launch phase)

The pillar is *established*, not just promised, by pointing early programming straight at it
while Relevé is getting up and running. During the launch / establishing phase, gear toward
financial literacy:

- **Live Pass events** built around financial literacy for dancers and dance families.
- **The Climb** content and issues oriented to this — the money reality, told honestly.
- **Live Zooms** aimed at exactly this, while getting established — a recurring place the
  community learns the floor together.

Do this **early and deliberately**, so financial literacy is understood as part of Relevé's
identity from the beginning — not something added on once the company is big. First
impressions set the pillar.

## The business-model fit (it pays the right way)

This obeys Relevé's own rule: **the demand side pays; talent never does.** Financial-wellness
organizations, credit unions, and youth-focused financial institutions **want** to reach this
audience — young, disciplined, career-minded performers and their families. **Sponsored
financial literacy is a demand-side revenue lane that funds the teaching so the dancer gets it
free** — the same shape as colleges paying to reach students. The dancer is never charged to
learn how to survive their own profession. Ever.

## Guardrails (so the mission stays clean)

1. **Education, never personalized financial or investment advice.** This is teaching, not
   advising. That line keeps the mission honest and keeps Relevé out of regulated territory.
2. **Minors live inside the guardian-consent container** already being built for minor data
   (COPPA / the Marshall Law legal review). Financial content for young people is
   guardian-managed and private.
3. **Core stable first.** Finish the floor of the *product* — auth, the three doors, the
   smooth experience — before layering the full financial module on top. Seed it with
   programming now; build the deep module once the base is solid.

## The line that must not be left out

**Relevé Connect doesn't just help dancers get found and get hired. It teaches them to keep
what they earn and build a life on an uneven year — because no one else ever did.** That is
the education pillar. It is integral, it is founding, and it belongs in the vision from here on.
