# Pricing, Professional Identity & Roster Visibility — Direction (FROM KATHLEEN)

*Direction for Claude Code. Updated 2026-08-08. Model: a two-sided dance network. **Free the side that CREATES the value (vetted professionals — the identity layer); monetize the side that CAPTURES it (demand + transactions).** Making the professional identity layer highly accessible is the growth engine, not a giveaway — it is the inventory the paying side pays to reach. Kathleen is compensated via the capture side, never by charging professionals to exist.*

## 1. The Professional Identity Layer — FREE & PUBLIC (highly accessible)
A vetted professional gets, at no cost, because these grow the network:
- **Public Relevé Professional Profile** (`/talent/<slug>`, shareable, no login to view). *(Already public — keep it.)*
- **Verified Professional status** — Relevé's vetting badge, shown publicly.
- **Public inclusion / discoverability** — findable, indexable (good for SEO and sharing).
- **A shareable Relevé URL** (e.g. `releveconnect.com/kathleen-mcaree`).
- **Ability to RECEIVE legitimate professional messages** (inbound is a free professional benefit).

These are the supply-side benefits. Do not paywall a professional's *existence, verification, discoverability, or ability to be contacted.*

## 2. Message quality guard (so "legitimate" holds)
Gate the **sender**, not the professional's inbox:
- A professional receives messages for free.
- Only **verified / known parties** may initiate (studios/employers with an account, or a light screen/verification on a first message).
- **Route all messaging through Relevé** — no exposed personal contact — to keep inbound high-quality, block spam, and prevent off-platform disintermediation.

## 3. Where revenue lives — the CAPTURE side (keep paid)
- **Demand / the hub:** studio subscriptions for the This Week + family hub + roster/schedule tools (and family features where they fit).
- **Transactions (fees on money moving):** choreography licensing / the Beat marketplace; Swing placements.
- **Optional premium PROFESSIONAL tiers** layered *on top of* the free identity layer — featured placement, analytics, marketplace payouts. Upgrades, never the price of admission.

## 4. The Roster (`/roster`) — browse vs. tools
- Individual **profiles** are public (§1). Discovery is satisfied by public profiles, so it does not depend on opening the whole Roster.
- Lean **open/freemium** on *browsing* the directory (supports discovery), but keep **bulk/power + outbound tooling** premium: advanced search/filter at scale, saved shortlists, outbound contact volume, exports. (Current build gates the whole Roster behind `hasAnyActiveMembership`; loosen toward "browse open / act = gated" only as a deliberate step.)
- The Verified badge, the Swing, scheduling, and payments stay inside Relevé (the profile is the hook; the platform is the value).

## 5. Founding-studio rate copy (Claude Code is on this now)
Reframe vague "founding rate" as a **time-boxed founder perk that names an end date and a real price**:

> **Founding Studios join free through December 31, 2026** — then our standard studio membership.

Always pair "free" with the end date + that a paid rate follows. Insert the real number where `[standard studio membership rate]` belongs once Kathleen sets it. Apply consistently on `/studios/join`, the invitation email, and the admin "complimentary membership" note.

*Final pricing numbers, exact sender-verification rules, and how far to open the Roster browse are Kathleen's calls — this doc records the model and reasoning so the build stays consistent.*
